MicScript Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

MicScript records, transcribes, and translates offline on your device. The app has no internet permission. We do not collect your recordings, text, or usage data. There are no accounts, ads, or analytics.

Microphone access is used for recording. Audio and text stay in private app storage until you delete them, unless you choose to share them. Cloud backup is disabled. Delete recordings in History, or clear app data or uninstall to remove all local data.

Google Play uses the internet for model downloads and Pro purchases under its Privacy Policy. Trial and purchase information is stored locally to manage access. The app does not receive payment card details or send audio or text to Google Play.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email support@neravo.cc. We use the information you send to answer your request.
